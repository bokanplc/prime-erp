How commission overrides work

        
A Senior Rep's manager automatically earns a 10% override on that rep's earned commission. A Junior Rep's manager earns a 25% override, and their assigned Senior Rep earns a 15% override — both calculated from the Junior's own earned commission, across all product lines. Overrides are computed automatically below; nothing needs to be entered twice.
